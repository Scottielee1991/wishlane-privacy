This policy describes the Android game Wish Lane: Cozy Logic Puzzles. Developer: Small Hours Software. Privacy contact: smallhourssoftware@proton.me.

Information kept on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, daily stamps, earned keys, settings, and ad-related state on your device. It also stores your chosen age range for advertising settings; it does not ask for your exact date of birth. The current app has no user account or developer-operated cloud progress sync. Your progress normally stays until you clear app data or uninstall the app; clearing it removes your local progress.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising consent where applicable. Google and any configured ad partners may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, device information, diagnostic data, and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalisation. The data and recipients depend on your device, location, choices and the developer's ad configuration. Even ads that are not personalised may involve data needed to deliver and secure the ad.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

In the app, open Settings and choose Advertising & privacy choices to review available consent options or change your age range. Available consent choices depend on your location. You can also manage advertising ID settings in Android's privacy settings. Ads are disabled in the app if no age range is selected or if the under-13 range is selected. Teen selections receive age-restricted treatment.

Sharing, retention and security

Advertising and consent information may be shared with Google and advertising partners according to their services and the choices available to you. Google and its partners have their own retention and deletion practices; see their privacy policies. The app stores local game data on the device and uses HTTPS for network connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children and teenagers

Wish Lane is intended for teenagers and adults. If an under-13 age range is selected, the app disables ad requests. Parents or guardians with questions may contact the developer using the contact below.

Privacy requests and contact

To ask about access, deletion or other privacy rights, contact smallhourssoftware@proton.me. You can remove local game data using your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. For information held by Google, see Google's privacy policy and its privacy controls.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Last updated: 27 October 2026.
